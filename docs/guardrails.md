# Scaling Guardrails — Memorial Day 2026

> **Context:** B2G2 sale. A true 2.0x Shopify MER is a *healthy* result if order volume is strong and Amazon is adding incremental revenue on top. Don't treat 2.0x as a weak hold zone.

> **Decision framework:** Shopify MER is the paid media decision metric. Total MER (Shopify + Amazon) is useful for broader business reporting, but it does not drive media-buying decisions.

The dashboard renders these live from `data/guardrails.json`.

## Scale Up
| Signal | Response |
|---|---|
| **Shopify MER 2.1x+** with stable or growing order volume | Clear scale-up. Increase total daily budget 10%–20% next day. |
| **Meta ROAS 1.8x–2.0x+** with stable CPA | Scale-up territory for Meta. Increase Meta budgets 10%–15%. |
| **Google ROAS 4.0x+** with room to spend | Increase Google budgets or loosen caps. |
| AOV holds **$72+** despite B2G2 | Lean into spend — offer is converting without trashing basket size. |

## Hold / Watch
| Signal | Response |
|---|---|
| **Shopify MER 1.8x–2.1x** | Hold/watch. Decision depends on AOV, CVR, and contribution margin. If margin is healthy, lean toward holding; rotate creative and monitor. |
| **Meta ROAS 1.5x–1.8x** and blended MER is healthy | Hold/optimize. Don't pull Meta back if blended Shopify MER is healthy. Shift incremental dollars where they perform. |
| AOV $68–$70 with strong order volume | Hold if contribution margin still works. Volume can carry a softer AOV. |

## Pull Back
| Signal | Response |
|---|---|
| **Shopify MER below 1.7x** for a full day | Pullback zone. Cut next-day planned increase by 15%–25% and re-evaluate. |
| **Meta ROAS below 1.4x–1.5x** with rising CPA | Pull Meta back or pause weakest ad sets/creatives. |
| CVR below 2.0% during sale | Funnel / offer-clarity warning. Check offer comprehension, landing page, cart, discount application — not necessarily a budget cut. |
| AOV below $66 | Margin check. Re-confirm contribution margin and whether 5 for $99 is cannibalizing the B2G2 path. |

## Risks
| Risk | Why | Mitigation | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| B2G2 mechanic not understood | Customers may not add 4 bands and may not see discount | Message: Add 4 bands to cart. Pay for 2. Use cart reminder. | Site + Creative |
| 5 for $99 confusion | Customers may not know which offer is better | Make B2G2 dominant; keep 5 for $99 secondary | Site + CX |
| Margin impact | Revenue ROAS can look good while profit tightens | Monitor CM, AOV, units/order, discount rate | Finance / Media |
| Inventory pressure | Best sellers can sell through from Shopify + Amazon demand | Daily inventory check; redirect traffic if hero SKUs run low | Ops / Media |
| Amazon over-credit on media decisions | Total MER may look better than Shopify media performance | Use Shopify MER for media decisions; Amazon is incremental reporting only | Media |
| Landing page friction | All-products path may reduce cart completion | Prefer dedicated Memorial Day sale collection / best sellers page | Site |
