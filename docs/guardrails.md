# Scaling Guardrails

The dashboard renders these live. Source of truth is `data/guardrails.json`.

## Scale Up
| Signal | Response |
|---|---|
| Shopify MER 2.2x+ and CVR 2.4%+ | Increase total daily budget 10%–20% next day |
| Meta ROAS 2.0x+ and CPA stable | Increase Meta budgets 10%–15% |
| Google ROAS 4.5x+ with room to spend | Increase Google budgets or loosen caps |
| AOV holds $72+ despite B2G2 | Lean into spend |

## Hold
| Signal | Response |
|---|---|
| Shopify MER 1.9x–2.2x | Hold budgets; rotate creative and monitor |
| Meta ROAS 1.7x–2.0x | Hold or shift incremental dollars to Google |
| AOV $68–$70 but orders are strong | Hold if contribution margin still works |

## Pull Back
| Signal | Response |
|---|---|
| Shopify MER below 1.8x for a full day | Cut next-day planned increase by 15%–25% |
| Meta ROAS below 1.6x and CPA rises | Pull Meta back or pause weakest ad sets/creatives |
| CVR below 2.0% during sale | Check offer clarity, landing page, cart, and discount application |
| AOV below $66 | Re-check margin and whether 5 for $99 is confusing the B2G2 path |

## Risks
| Risk | Why | Mitigation | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| B2G2 mechanic not understood | Customers may not add 4 bands and may not see discount | Message: Add 4 bands to cart. Pay for 2. Use cart reminder. | Site + Creative |
| 5 for $99 confusion | Customers may not know which offer is better | Make B2G2 dominant; keep 5 for $99 secondary | Site + CX |
| Margin impact | Revenue ROAS can look good while profit tightens | Monitor CM, AOV, units/order, discount rate | Finance / Media |
| Inventory pressure | Best sellers can sell through from Shopify + Amazon demand | Daily inventory check; redirect traffic if hero SKUs run low | Ops / Media |
| Amazon over-credit | Total MER may look better than Shopify media performance | Use Shopify MER for media decisions; report Amazon separately | Media |
| Landing page friction | All-products path may reduce cart completion | Prefer dedicated Memorial Day sale collection / best sellers page | Site |
