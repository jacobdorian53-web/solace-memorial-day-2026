How We Measure

  • Primary paid media metric: Shopify MER (Shopify revenue ÷ paid spend). This drives all media-buying decisions.
  • Total business metric: Total MER ((Shopify + Amazon) ÷ paid spend). Reported separately.
  • Amazon treatment: Shown as a separate additive line. Base model = 8% of Shopify revenue. Do not use Amazon to justify Meta scaling.
  • Working plan: Moderate scenario unless margin, inventory, or offer clarity say otherwise.

Key Points for the Team

  1. Use Shopify MER for media-buying decisions. Use Shopify + Amazon MER only for total business reporting.
  2. Amazon is shown as a separate additive line. Base model uses 8% of Shopify revenue (6% low / 10% high).
  3. Start with the moderate plan unless margin, inventory, or offer comprehension says otherwise.
  4. Do not let Amazon revenue alone justify aggressive Meta scaling if Shopify MER is weak.
  5. The biggest execution detail is offer clarity: Add 4 bands to cart. Pay for 2.
